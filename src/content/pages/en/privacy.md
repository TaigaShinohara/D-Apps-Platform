## What this site does not collect

There are no accounts, contact forms, uploads, advertising, or visitor analytics. We do not request names or email addresses, or save visitor access logs or browsing histories in storage operated by D-Apps.

We do not use cookies, localStorage, or identifiers for tracking. Language selection is part of the URL and is not saved. Images and styles are served locally, without third-party fonts or embedded videos.

## Processing needed to deliver the site

AWS delivers pages and download files. AWS necessarily processes IP addresses and request information to provide network communication and defend against abuse. This does not mean that no information is ever processed on the internet. See the [AWS Privacy Notice](https://aws.amazon.com/privacy/) for AWS’s own practices.

D-Apps is configured to disable CloudFront and S3 access logs and WAF request logging and sampling. Monitoring uses aggregate traffic and error metrics. Administrator audit records are separate from visitor tracking.

## External sites and downloaded apps

Stores, GitHub, and support destinations are external services. Their own privacy policies apply after you follow a link. We do not embed those sites into these pages.

Downloaded apps have their own data practices, explained on their detail pages. Do not assume this website’s policy describes every app.

## Changes

Any proposal to collect more data requires a review of this policy and the site design before implementation.

Effective date: November 16, 2026.
